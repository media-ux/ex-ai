§ 05

Voice. How EX · AI sounds.

Three audiences, one voice. Adjust register, not character.

01 Authoritative

We name what we know, and we name what we don't. No hedging either way. If a result is 94.2%, that's what we print — not "world-class".

✓ "Accuracy: 94.2% on MMLU-Pro."
✗ "Groundbreaking, best-in-class performance."

02 Warm

When we speak to students, parents and first-time customers, we slow down. Short sentences. Real nouns. No jargon unless we define it one line later.

✓ "You'll learn to build the model, then to break it."
✗ "Leverage SOTA architectures across the stack."

03 Confident

Short verbs. Active voice. We describe what we've done or are doing now, with a date attached.

✓ "Shipped to 14 research labs in 2025."
✗ "Looking forward to potentially partnering."

§ 07

Family alignment & final notes.

One palette, four entities, one signature.

Parent alignment

EX · AI adopts the EX Venture parent palette — ink, cream, golden hour, ember, moss — so the four infrastructure entities (Venture, AI, Fi, Academy) read as siblings. Differentiation lives in typography and mark construction, not in color.

Recommendation

Primary lockup: Direction A · Monolith in Ink + Golden Hour — the executive face of intelligence infrastructure. Secondary: Direction B · Signal for the console, Direction C · Atlas for Academy and long-form editorial. The interpunct EX · AI is the primary treatment — matching parent naming; the spaced EX AI is used in body copy and titles.
